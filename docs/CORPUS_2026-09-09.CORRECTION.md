## What was measured on 2026-10-17

The catalogue `private/fb_pre_july/catalog.csv` lists 20 reels, dated 2026-06-14 to 2026-07-04. Each was opened at `facebook.com/reel/<id>` in a browser with no Facebook sign-in.

- **All 20 show the audience "Public"** to a logged-out visitor.
- Four first showed a login wall. All four showed "Public" when reopened after a 4-second wait.
- The captions shown include "If something happens to me i think i did something" (14 June), "Pretty sure i proved consciousness foreal" (18 June) and "Proof of work/concept." (26 June).

**What a reader can check.** The catalogue in `private/` is never published, and a reader cannot open it. The finding does not depend on it, though: it rests on public pages. Any of the operator's Facebook reels from those dates can be opened while signed out, and the audience label read for yourself.

## Settled by the operator, later the same day

His words: "I made them public after that date." So the original was **accurate when written**. On 2026-09-09 the videos were "Only me", and he made them public afterwards.

- **Before and through the 6 July announcement** they were not public.
- **Their dates still stand.** Facebook gives each reel an upload date, and every date here is before the announcement. What they show is that the material was *recorded* before July. They do not show it was *published* before July.

The two subsections below were written before he answered. They are kept as written. His answer settles the first point.

## What this does not settle

- **When they became public.** Facebook does not show a post's audience history. The original's "Only me" may have been true on 2026-09-09 and changed later, or it may have been wrong when written. Either way, the operator states they are public, and the measurement agrees.
- **The other 6 of the 26.** The catalogue measured here holds 20 rows. The remaining 6 of the 26 videos in 23 upload events (some posts carry more than one video) were not opened.
