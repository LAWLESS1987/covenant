## The commitment

**The held core is not to be altered silently, by anyone — its author included.
No override.**

## The honest limit, which is part of the commitment, not an escape from it

A line of code cannot bind the person who holds root. A guard with no bypass can
still be deleted by the one hand that owns the tree. To claim otherwise — to
promise a lock that cannot exist — would itself be the first alteration of the
honest core, a false comfort it forbids. So this record does not pretend to a
power it lacks.

The only true *no override* is the one no single hand can reach: **hashed,
published, witnessed, and held by more than one.** That is why the covenant is
built to outlive whoever made it, and why "refinements only until there are more
voices" exists. A commitment only one person can revoke is not kept. One that is
hashed, dated, and held by a quorum is.
