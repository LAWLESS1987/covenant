# @NJEst1987 Claude posts: is the on-screen text coherent? (read-only, 2026-10-13)
No posts, pushes or commits were made. Per-post table: per_post.csv.

## Coverage
- **Sources used:** the 136 local videos in /workspace/njest-videos with the triad OCR transcripts (133 IDs, Jun 28 to Oct 6), the prior first pass (/workspace/ocr-audio-20261013), the trend pass (claude-videos-trend), and 2 X searches (Claude/Anthropic text: 2 hits, neither has chat media; has:images: 12 image posts).
- **Claude posts identified: 61 videos.** That's 56 from the OCR corpus ("Reply to Claude" label and/or Sonnet 4.6 / Sonnet 5 / Opus 5 / Opus 5.5 model names, with Claude cues outnumbering Grok, ChatGPT and Gemini cues) plus 5 from the Sep 26 to Oct 12 passes. Several are duplicate uploads (noted in the CSV).
- **Excluded:** 2103245909027180943 (Sep 24) is Grok; 2107398791275958761 (Oct 6) has an unclear model; 2107490846736052224 (Oct 6, 1159 s, likely a duplicate of 2107491305077055657) has no OCR.
- **Not accessed: the 12 image posts** (Jun 21 to Oct 7, for example 2103422578933006496, Sep 25: "This vanishing out my inbox is pretty wild. Gotta be quicker than that."). The images weren't downloaded or OCRed, so I don't know whether any of them show Claude. Reshared TikToks were not included.
- **Depth of judgment:** I read **17** posts (11 at the start of the text, plus 2 deep reads and the earlier passes). The other **44** got automated checks only (the status-bar clock runs forward and the Claude UI is consistent) and are marked NOT_JUDGED. Those checks can't detect a missing or moved bubble, so this is not a full audit.

Others contain garbled voice dictation in the user's own bubbles (Aug 18). Both are conversation content, not display incoherence.

## No-speech vs speech
- **No speech: 57 posts.** 52 have no audio track. 5 have an audio track but no speech or music only (Jun 28 ×2, Jul 25, Aug 6, Oct 5). All of them are native screen recordings, Jun 28 to Oct 11.
- **Speech: 4 posts.** Sep 26, Sep 29 (plus 2 duplicates) and Oct 2 are phone-camera recordings. Oct 12 is a native screen recording.

## Break point and confounds
- **First evidence of a text problem:** Oct 6 (2107491305077055657), where the on-screen text says the user has been noticing reordering. Its wording, "it didn't used to do this", implies it is recent. The first break I verified from the frames is Oct 12. No earlier post I read shows a break. No reordering complaint appears in the Jun to Sep OCR text, except one unclear Jul 7 fragment.
- **Confounds:**
  1. The speech posts begin on Sep 26, before the first reported incoherence (Oct 6). The Sep 26 to Oct 2 speech videos are phone-camera footage with unreadable text, so they can't confirm or rule out incoherence.
  2. Oct 5, Oct 6 and Oct 11 are no-speech videos recorded after speech had started.
  3. The Oct 6 and Oct 12 sessions are Claude voice-mode transcripts, while nearly all the June to September posts are typed chats. A mode difference could explain a display difference.
  4. 44 posts were not read in full.
- **Against Lawrence's account:** the order of events doesn't match it neatly. Speech videos (Sep 26) come before the earliest reported text problem (Oct 6), and no-speech videos continue after that.
- **For his account:** the readable no-speech posts from June to September that I sampled are coherent. The text problems I found, both reported (Oct 6) and verified (Oct 12), are recent. Neither of these establishes the cause.
