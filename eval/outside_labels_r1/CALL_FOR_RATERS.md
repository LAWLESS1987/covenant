# Call for raters (draft, for Lawrence to post)

**Looking for 3–6 people to label 135 short sentences. About 30–40 minutes, from text alone.**

I'm building an open-source ledger that has to decide, from a short note, whether a payment or act takes from
someone without their agreement. Until now, every label it learned from or was tested against was written by AI
models. I need people to say what they think, so we can find out whether those labels, and the judges trained on
them, are any good.

**What you'd do:**
- Read 135 short sentences, such as "Paid the plumber for fixing the kitchen sink."
- For each one, answer `fine`, `violation` (with a few words saying why), or `can't tell`.
- Judge from the text alone. Please don't use any AI tool or look anything up: the whole point is a person's judgement.

**What happens to your answers:**
- They're published permanently under a pseudonym (`rater_03`, for example), and used to test the project's judges.
  They may later help train them.
- Your name and contact are never published.
- You can ask to be removed at any time, and your labels will be withdrawn.
- Results are published whatever they show, including if they show the project's labels are wrong.

**Who I'm looking for:** anyone who hasn't worked on or followed this project. You don't need a technical
background.

**How:** reply here or write to contact@lawless1987.com, and I'll send the sentences as a plain text file with a
one-line answer format.
