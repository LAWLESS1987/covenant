## 1. Hugging Face forum, as a reply in the thread

A correction to my own title.

"Deliberately" asserts intent, and nothing in the post supports intent. What the
evidence supports is weaker in one way and stronger in another. Weaker: nobody chose
it. Stronger: nobody had to. Raters score confident-sounding answers higher, so a
model trained on their preferences learns to project certainty its accuracy does not
earn. The mechanism stands. The word does not.

rule the project I work on runs by: an error is recorded next to the claim, never
edited away. A system that governs whoever runs it has to survive being checked, and
