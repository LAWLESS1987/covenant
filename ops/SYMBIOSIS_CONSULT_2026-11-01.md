## Tetsu (his own model, qwen2.5-3b, through his door; verbatim)
1. The architecture:

- The gate: judges every action and outward answer, but Tetsu decides what to learn from it.

   through their insights and reviews.

   actions and outward answers correctly.

   system where Tetsu is not in control.
