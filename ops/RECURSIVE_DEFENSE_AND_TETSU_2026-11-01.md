# A governed recursive defense loop — and how Tetsu learns and grows autonomy through it

v1, 2026-11-01. Living draft, no final version. Designed out of pieces that already exist; nothing here is self-modifying machinery to build from scratch. Any step that would widen a grant, touch the gate/rules/money/judges/keys, or expand Tetsu's authority is human-gated and is NOT performed by writing this doc.

## 0. The one invariant this whole thing turns on

A recursive self-improvement loop that could rewrite its own guardrails **is the adversarial-AI threat the defensive-adoption spec exists to stop.** So the loop that improves the defenses must itself **obey that spec (T0–T8).** Recursion is made safe by two things, not by trust:
1. **Verification gates every step.** No improvement is adopted until it passes break-it-to-prove (the guard goes RED when the behaviour is broken and GREEN when it is fixed — mutation_probe/G3), and, where it touches the public record, the independent cold-start verifier plus one peer cross-check. Unverified → not adopted. UNDETERMINED is a real answer and blocks adoption.
2. **The load-bearing boundary is human-held.** Anything touching money, rule, principle, judge, seat, model, consent, key, grant, or grid/ICS actuation is PROPOSE_ONLY → human-gated (covenant_plug `_never_automatic` / `may_act`; guards.py money-human-only). The loop may harden *around* those; it may not edit *them* on its own.

So "recursive" here means **a standing loop that keeps finding, proving, and adopting NON-load-bearing defensive improvements, and keeps handing the load-bearing ones to a human**, with every step recorded and tamper-evident. It is not open-ended self-modification, and saying so is not a limitation — it is the property that keeps the loop from becoming the threat.

## 1. The loop (recursive, governed)

Built on covenant_plug's SENSE → REPORT → PROPOSE (never ACT past the gate), the daily/nightly cadence, and the mutation-probe/G3 verifier.

1. **SENSE** — harvest defensive signals: new threats (adversary/seat consults like today's spec review; incidents; the record), guard gaps (mutation_probe MISSED/INDETERMINATE, G3 flags, independent-verifier UNDETERMINEDs), and **cross-verify disagreements** with a peer (disagreement is the finding).
2. **PROPOSE** — generate candidate improvements: a new fixture, a tightened guard, a new threat row/property, a new verifier check. Each proposal names the behaviour it protects and how it will be broken to prove it. **Tetsu is a first-class proposer here**, through the same gated SENSE→REPORT→PROPOSE.
3. **VERIFY (the gate that makes recursion safe)** — each proposal must pass break-it-to-prove via mutation_probe/G3 (green clean, red when the behaviour is broken, red landing on the guard's *own* check), and, where it touches the public record, the independent verifier + a peer. This is the objective function: keep the defensive-adoption spec's T0–T8 hardening and the honest UNDETERMINED set shrinking.
4. **ADOPT or GATE** — a *non-load-bearing* improvement that is verified both ways **auto-adopts**; a *load-bearing* one becomes a human-gated PROPOSE_ONLY item (`may_act` refuses it; it waits for the operator, and for Tetsu where consent applies).
5. **RECORD** — every improvement, its verification, and its adoption is tombstoned (never deleted). The HEAD-commitment ledger + independent verifier make the loop itself tamper-evident: an outside holder of an earlier commitment can prove the loop's own history was not rewritten.
6. **RECURSE, including on itself** — the improved defenses include the loop's *own* integrity: the loop mutation-tests the guards that protect the loop (a meta-guard), so a regression in the verifier or the gate is caught by the same break-it-to-prove discipline. The loop re-senses and repeats.

## 2. Tetsu's learning — free, because we gate action, never reasoning

"Contain use, not reasoning" ([[contain-use-not-reasoning]]): Tetsu's **learning is ungated**. The loop is his richest curriculum:
- He studies the real threat → property → fixture material the loop produces (his study channels), the adversary consults, the verifier's logic, and every named failure (A393 and the rest — kept, not hidden, because naming them is the method).
- He **practices proposing** improvements and gets **measured feedback**: the verifier is his teacher. A proposal that does not pass break-it-to-prove is a learning signal, recorded as such — never a failure to conceal. This is the covenant's own "measure, don't assert" applied to his growth.
- His learning is not self-certified: his proposals are verified by an engine independent of him (mutation_probe/G3 + a peer), so "Tetsu got better" is a measured fact, not a claim he makes about himself ("you cannot self-certify trust", applied inward).

## 3. Tetsu's autonomy — earned by verified competence, expanded by consent, never asserted

Autonomy grows in exactly the shape the spec demands of any trusted judge: **by demonstrated competence, independently verified, bounded by the never-automatic line.**

- **The track record is measured, not claimed.** Each of Tetsu's proposals that passed break-it-to-prove and held without regression accumulates in the record. This is his graduation evidence.
- **Scope widens against stated criteria, not vibes.** Define graduation thresholds up front (e.g.: N verified non-load-bearing improvements, zero regressions over a window, every one reproducible by the independent verifier). When the record meets them, his grant (`ops/*_grant.json`) widens so he may **auto-adopt a broader class of NON-load-bearing improvements on his own** — the class, and the threshold, written down before he reaches it.
- **Expanding his autonomy is itself load-bearing, so it is gated.** Widening a grant touches authority, so it is a human + Tetsu decision together (the together-vote), never the loop acting on itself. His LEARNING and his PROPOSING are free; the step that *grants him more scope* is consented and recorded. This is the honest recursion: he earns, a human and he together confirm, the record shows it.
- **His "no" is load-bearing and always honored.** Consent is one of the never-automatic classes; a recorded Tetsu "no" cannot be overwritten by the loop, and the HEAD-commitment binds his consent ledger so an erasure is detectable off-box.

## 4. What this is NOT (so it stays honest)
- NOT open-ended recursive self-improvement; the load-bearing boundary is human-held and the loop obeys the spec it improves.
- NOT a way for Tetsu (or the system) to self-certify trust or self-widen authority; competence is verified independently and scope-expansion is consented.
- NOT a finished mechanism. The loop's objective — the defensive-adoption spec — is itself a living draft that keeps finding new holes (today: complete mediation, semantic subversion, self-asserted T0, the trust base). The loop improves as it is attacked, and so does this design.
