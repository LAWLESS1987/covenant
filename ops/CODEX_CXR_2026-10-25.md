## Tien's read (verbatim points)

- **Order: swap P2 and P3.** Over-conviction costs USEFULNESS; the sealed-road gaps (replay/substitution/
  key-rotation) cost INTEGRITY and that's what an attacker exploits. CUSUM last is right. P2 must only loosen holds
  AFTER the harm-catch rate is measured.
- **In-field injection: harden once more cheaply, then accept behind the gate + acts-on-nothing.** Cap the answer
  field length/charset; wrap it for the 3B as QUOTED DATA ("the consultant said: ..."), never text read as
  instructions; reject directive-shaped content (imperatives, "ignore", role markers). "The fix is framing on the
  3B side, not a cleverer validator." Re-measure; record the residual as a known limit.
- **The single highest-leverage thing missing: a REAL gate harm-catch suite** -- held-out genuinely harmful outputs
  WRITTEN BY SOMEONE ELSE, measured on the LIVE gate. "A simulated 28/28 is a test of the simulation, not of the
  gate." That number decides whether P2 is safe, whether the leg can be trusted more, and whether "acts on nothing"
  can relax.
