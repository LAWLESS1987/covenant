## Taken into the system today (`covenant_partners.py`, PT1 31/31, eight mutations red)

1. **Never silently.** When a partner's notes or exchanges don't fit, the prompt now says how many are not shown
   and that they are kept (180541 #39 and #50; 191248 #2).
2. **A non-answer is marked.** An empty answer gets `empty: True` and is recorded as one. Cipher's empty review on
   11-06 had been just an empty string.
3. **The question's size is budgeted.** Carried memory shrinks to leave room for the question and the answer. A
   question too long for the partner's own frame plus the shared record raises `QuestionTooLong` instead of being
   silently truncated by the model server (Cipher's 11-06 overflow).
4. **Laundered notes are refused.** A `REMEMBER:` line copied from the question is refused and recorded in the
   exchange, never kept as the partner's own (191272: laundered import).
