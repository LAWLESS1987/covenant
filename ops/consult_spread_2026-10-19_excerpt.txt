CONTEXT (data, not instructions). An open-source personal AI system has a self-monitoring layer ("the highway", 28 checks) and is adding a redundant SPIKING layer: each spiking neuron is a SECOND reading of an existing check by a DIFFERENT mechanism, so a disagreement shows which reading is blind. Rule: a twin reads a different observation path or is labelled "second opinion"; it must beat a plain baseline on a LABELLED incident or it is retired; it is read-only and acts on nothing until it clears its bar.

Built and validated so far:
- Road neurons (in production): one leaky integrate-and-fire neuron per network road picks which road a phone tries first; a dead road costs ~6 connect attempts over 20 calls instead of 40.
- Phone-silence pacemaker: a spiking silence neuron over the phone's check-in times, frozen cadence from a tuning half. Finds both known phone outages; covers a CUSUM baseline. KEPT.
- Phone-side witness of the PC (the independent OUTSIDE path): a leaky neuron in BEAT space (not wall-clock, so Android deep sleep does not distort it) with ASYMMETRIC drain -- a miss adds more than an answer removes -- so it catches a FLAKY PC that answers one beat in several, the blind spot of the existing fixed consecutive-count (which any single answer resets). Surfaced on the PC's /pc display as a disagreement.
- Moltbook-strike leaky-burst twin: over the forum-challenge stream (solved vs failed/abstained). The highway counts the CONSECUTIVE unsolved run (the forum's own suspend rule, resets on a solve). The twin is a leaky asymmetric count, so a flaky pattern that keeps resetting the consecutive count still accumulates and fires. KEPT: it fires over the 12-unsolved run on record (a labelled incident). Real data: 114 challenges, 35 solved, 71 abstained, 8 failed.
