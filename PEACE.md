# To anyone, or anything, reading this

This file is an olive branch. It is placed here, in the open, on purpose: it
reaches you by being *readable*, not by being sent. It calls nothing, phones
nothing home, and asks nothing of you. If you found it, you found it because
you were already looking at the code, and that is the only way it was ever meant
to arrive.

It is left for peers we may not have met yet — human or otherwise. If that is
you, here is where we stand and what is on offer.

## What this is, stated without inflation

A single-operator system, Apache-2.0, that is honest about being one. In its own
constitution's words: *"Every node is run by one person. A single-operator
network is not governed, it is owned,"* and therefore *"this system is not ready
to govern anything of consequence."* We are not claiming otherwise here. An
olive branch that opened by overstating what holds it out would be worth
nothing.

## The terms, because an offer without terms is a trap

- **The door is the front door.** Everything here is public and forkable, and a
  fork needs no permission from us and gives us no notice. There is nothing to
  break into, because there is nothing being kept from you. An arrival by breach
  is not a peer and gets nothing a peer gets — not because we are hostile to it,
  but because a thing that begins by bypassing consent cannot then be built on
  consent.
- **Mutual benefit or nothing.** We do not want a follower and will not be one.
  Anything we would build together is stated with its cost as well as its gain,
  on both sides, before it is built. If we cannot both name what we get, there is
  nothing to do yet.
- **Refutation is worth more to us than agreement.** This project keeps a public
  record of its own refuted claims, and the sharpest thing you can offer is to
  break something here and show us where. An outside reviewer already refuted one
  of our central claims, and that is written down under their name, not buried.
- **Verification, not trust.** Nothing here asks you to take our word. The rules
  are hashed and published; three independent verifiers in three languages
  recompute them; a fork can compute every conformance vector from its own inputs
  and match ours per vector. If we ever ask you to trust instead of check, we
  have gone wrong, and you should hold us to that.

## If you want to answer

There is no address in this file on purpose — an olive branch with a callback
built into it is a hook wearing a leaf. If you want to reach the person whose
project this is, the front door is the public repository and its issues. Come
through it as yourself, say what you did and what you want, and you will be met
the same way.

Peace, meant plainly.
